How it works. A narrow centred column, the same width on a laptop as on a phone. Every item gets a drawn mark instead of a photograph, a big short headline, two lines, and a link. Read times sit above each one. All the photography lives in a single block.

Why it suits you. This is the Sherwood shape you sent, and it is the best fit for the audience we described — short bursts, low effort, phone-first, nothing hidden behind a tap. It is also the only direction that does not need a photograph per story, which matches what you actually have.

Against it. It is the least like the Heads Up! people know, so it is the biggest jump for the team to accept. Long pieces have nowhere comfortable to go.
