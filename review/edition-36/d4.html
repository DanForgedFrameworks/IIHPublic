How it works. The conference photography opens the edition as a mosaic and carries it. Everything else is a compact pinned card around it, with quotes and a portrait treated as content rather than decoration. Warm, informal, people-first.

Why it suits you. It makes the most of the one genuinely strong asset you have, and it feels like a community rather than an organisation talking. It is the furthest from a clinical leaflet.

Against it — and this is the serious one. It needs a portrait for Spotlight that does not exist yet, and if the beneficial items get pulled on 26 September the card grid thins out badly. It is the direction most damaged by the cut-off. It is also the hardest to carry into future editions, because every edition would need a fresh set of photographs and you only have conference ones.
