How it works. One story leads and owns the only big photograph. Two secondaries sit beneath it, then the conference photo grid, then the small stuff as briefs. Standing items — membership, volunteering, support — live in a rail so they never compete with news.

Why it suits you. It needs photography in exactly two places, which is exactly what you have. Hierarchy tells a tired reader what matters without them deciding.

Against it. It is the most conventional of the four. It will read as "a newsletter" rather than as something new.
