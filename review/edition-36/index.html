Conference · booking open

June 2027 is open

When
Sat 12 June 2027
Where
Village Hotel, Solihull
Tickets
£25

A full day, 9am to 5pm, of lived experience, current treatment and research, and space for honest questions. The £25 is subsidised by IIH UK and includes all refreshments and lunch.

You need to be an IIH UK member to come. Not a member yet? Join first, then book — membership starts at £5 a year for one person, or £10 for a family.

Anyone 14 and over needs a ticket. Under-13s are free, but let Betsy know they are coming at treasurer@iih.org.uk (opens your email app).

Worth knowing
Research

The research that reaches space

Astronauts can develop eye and pressure changes that look like IIH. So the Birmingham team led by Professor Alexandra Sinclair now works with NASA — and what is learned in orbit feeds back into IIH care.

Running now
HOPE Programme

The autumn course has started

The Hope Programme for IIH is a six-session online course, created with IIH UK, people living with IIH and healthcare professionals. The current course began on 16 September.

There are no further courses planned yet. We will share dates in Heads Up! as soon as there are.
