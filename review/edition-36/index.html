Heads Up! is coming back

Two drafts · four short questions · about ten minutes

I am rebuilding the newsletter so it works on a phone, can be read in short bursts, and does not blind you on a bad day. Before I write the words, I want to get the shape right — and I would rather hear from you now than after it is built.

The words are the same in both drafts. The only difference is how the edition starts. Have a look at each, ideally on your phone.

Draft A

Opens with a picture

A photograph across the top, then the title, then straight into the stories. It feels like a magazine.

But you have to scroll past the picture before you can see what is in the edition.

Open Draft A
Draft B

Opens with a contents list

The title, then a numbered list of everything in the edition, so you can jump straight to what you want.

But it is a plainer start and feels less like a magazine.

Open Draft B

The four questions

One or two words is a perfect answer. You do not need to explain.

  1. A or B?
  2. Is the text big enough to read comfortably on your phone?
  3. Is anything too much — too busy, too bright, too many things at once?
  4. Is there anything you expected to find and could not?

Please reply by Wednesday 17 September. After that I lock the layout and start writing. You are not being asked about the wording or whether the facts are right yet — that is the next round.

Things you can safely ignore.

Every photograph is a grey placeholder — the real conference pictures go in later. The Spotlight section is deliberately blank until someone agrees to be featured. None of the buttons do anything yet.

One thing that does work, and is worth trying: the Warm / Dark / Bright buttons at the very top. It opens on Warm, because a white screen is the thing people tell me they struggle with most on a bad day. Tell me if it helps, or if you would rather it started bright.

What this is

A draft of Heads Up! Edition 36, for review by the IIH UK team. Not the finished newsletter.
