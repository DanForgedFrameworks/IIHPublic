Heads Up! is coming back

Four shapes · four short questions · about ten minutes

I am rebuilding the newsletter so it works properly on a phone, can be read in short bursts, and does not blind you on a bad day. Before I write a word, I want to get the shape right — and I would rather hear from you now than after it is built.

These are rough sketches, not finished designs. Grey boxes are where photographs go and grey bars are where the writing goes. There is no colour scheme yet on purpose — I am asking about the shape of the thing, not what it looks like painted.

Each one puts the same content in a different order. Use the buttons at the top to flip between them. Under each sketch there is a short note on how it works and what is wrong with it.

The four questions

One or two words is a perfect answer. You do not need to explain yourself.

  1. Which number?
  2. Is anything too much — too busy, too cluttered, too many things at once?
  3. Is there anything you expected to find and could not?
  4. Anything you would move, or want nearer the top?

Please reply by Wednesday 17 September. After that I settle the shape and start writing. You are not being asked about the wording or whether the facts are right yet — that comes next, and there will be a proper draft to read.

Things you can safely ignore.

Nothing is clickable inside the sketches. The photographs are grey boxes. The writing is grey bars, except the headlines, which are roughly what they will say. Colours, fonts and the finished look all come after you have told me which shape works.
