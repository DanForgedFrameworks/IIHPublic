Worth knowing
Research

The research that reaches space

Astronauts can develop eye and pressure changes that look like IIH. So the Birmingham team led by Professor Alex Sinclair now works with NASA — and what is learned in orbit feeds back into IIH care.

Read the Birmingham story (opens in a new tab)

Also: Amanda and Clare are excited to be working with an international team of researchers on developing new guidelines for IIH. We will share more with you as soon as we are able.

Programme update

HOPE and our new tools

Running now
HOPE Programme

The autumn course has started

The Hope Programme for IIH is a free six-session online course, created with IIH UK, people living with IIH and healthcare professionals. The current course began on 16 September.

A 2026 evaluation of 454 people across 11 courses found promising improvements in mental wellbeing. IIH UK funded this evaluation through one of its 2024 research grants to HOPE CIC.

There are no further courses planned yet. We will share dates in Heads Up! as soon as there are.
