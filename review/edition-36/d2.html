How it works. One big photograph up top, long-form writing in two columns, and a pull quote standing in wherever there is no photo. Section rubrics are typographic rules rather than coloured boxes. The conference becomes a photo essay near the end.

Why it suits you. It is the only direction that turns your quotes into imagery — and you have a lot of good quotes and almost no photographs outside the conference. It reads as a publication, not a bulletin.

Against it. Two-column text is hard on a phone and has to collapse to one, so desktop and mobile diverge more than the others. It also wants more words than you may get from a team with no newsletter editor.
